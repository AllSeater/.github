<h1 align="center">AllSeater</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Season-ticket management made simple.</strong></p>

AllSeater helps organizations put more tickets in more hands. Bring ticket
inventory into one place, distribute seats quickly and fairly, and give
participants a clear self-service experience from selection through claim.

## Make every seat count

- Organize ticket inventory, events, venues, and participants.
- Run fair, flexible ticket lotteries for teams and communities.
- Let participants express their preferences and claim tickets themselves.
- Track ticket outcomes so fewer seats go unused.

Learn more at [allseater.com](https://allseater.com), or contact us at
[support@allseater.com](mailto:support@allseater.com).
